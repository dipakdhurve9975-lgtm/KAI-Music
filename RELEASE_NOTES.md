## KAI Music preview

Complete Universal GMS debug-signed APK, Android 8.0+; app version 1.4.4 (165). This is an early public preview, not a production-signed store release.

- Immediate root navigation; selector feedback remains independent of destination composition.
- Original lyric text with optional Romanization/translation and five presentation styles.
- Genuine timestamp validation, millisecond parsing, precise active-line lookup, per-song offset/reset and reliable scroll follow.
- Untimed lyrics remain readable static text; timestamps are never invented.
- Bounded lyrics viewport separated from song information and playback controls.
- Shared Ambient Normal/Vinyl, vinyl rendering, Home dock hide/reveal and original KAI branding.
- Owner GitHub, Instagram, Buy Me a Coffee and UPI links.

Validation: Universal GMS build successful; 180 tests passed (160 app, 12 lyrics, 6 LRCLib, 2 YouLyPlus). Full on-phone regression testing was deferred by the owner. No measured navigation performance improvement is claimed from interrupted profiling runs.
